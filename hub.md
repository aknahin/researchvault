# ResearchVault

A curated Obsidian vault kit for PhD students and researchers: five templates
with locked front-matter, a Zotero-to-Obsidian citation pipeline (plugin path
plus a plugin-free CSV path with a bundled stdlib-only script), a
read-now/skim/defer/reject triage queue, an 8-prompt AI triage pack, and two
worked fake-paper examples. Setup takes about 15 minutes.

**Honest note:** free community vaults (bramses' Research OS, PaperBell,
zotlit) are genuinely good. ResearchVault is curation + documentation — one
documented pipeline you can adopt end to end instead of assembling one.

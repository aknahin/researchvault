# ResearchVault — The PhD & Researcher Obsidian Vault

**Get ResearchVault here: <https://nandminc.gumroad.com/l/researchvault-kit>** — $1 during the intro launch.

ResearchVault is a curated Obsidian vault kit for PhD students and researchers.
It documents one complete literature pipeline — from a Zotero export to a
thesis draft — as a folder of Markdown templates you open in Obsidian and start
using the same day.

## What it is

- **5 templates with locked front-matter** — literature note, paper index,
  concept note, thesis chapter scaffold, and meeting/supervisor note. The
  front-matter fields are fixed so your notes stay queryable.
- **A Zotero-to-Obsidian citation pipeline** — two documented paths: a plugin
  path, and a plugin-free CSV path that ships with a bundled stdlib-only script
  (no Python packages to install).
- **A read-now / skim / defer / reject triage queue** — so every paper that
  enters the vault gets an explicit decision instead of sitting in an inbox.
- **An 8-prompt AI triage pack** — copy-paste prompts for summarizing,
  comparing, and extracting claims from your notes.
- **Two worked fake-paper examples** — complete example notes showing the
  templates and the pipeline in use, without depending on any real paper.

Setup takes about **15 minutes**: download, open the folder as a vault, follow
the START HERE note, point the CSV script at a Zotero export.

## Honest note

Free community vaults already exist and are good — bramses' Research OS,
PaperBell, zotlit. ResearchVault is not a new idea; it is **curation plus
documentation**: one pipeline, written down end to end, that you can adopt
without assembling it yourself from forum posts.

## Who it's for

- PhD students (or incoming ones) who re-find the same paper three times.
- Researchers whose reading notes live in three tools and never reach the draft.
- Anyone who wants Obsidian + Zotero working as one pipeline, without a
  plugin-setup rabbit hole.
